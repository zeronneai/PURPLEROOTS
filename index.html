03 / 08Method

How we work.

Our method has an order, and it isn't for everyone. Ads come last, on purpose.

  1. 01 Research Before Creative

    Research the market

    Every brand starts with the market. We study your audience, your category, and your competitors before we make a single thing. Creative without research is just noise.

  2. 02 Earn Attention, Don't Rent It

    Earn the attention of the right audience

    We win the right audience with creative content and creative marketing, not by renting reach. Attention you earn compounds. Attention you buy disappears the day you stop paying.

  3. 03 Community Over Vanity

    Build community and die-hard fans with creative content

    Views feel good, but a community that shows up, shares, and buys is the real asset. We turn attention into die-hard fans, the kind who root for you.

  4. 04 Ads Amplify What Works

    Paid ads, only at the end

    Paid ads come after the community exists, not before. We scale what's already proven, so every dollar pours fuel on a fire that's already lit. They amplify. They never carry it.

07 / 08Honesty

Are we the right fit?

We're going to be honest with you, because we'd rather lose a prospect than waste both our time. Our method has an order, and it isn't for everyone.

We're probably not for you if:

  • You want to pour money into ads before you have an audience
  • Your budget is "we'll see what you quote"
  • You think one boosted post is a strategy
  • You want reach today and don't care if it lasts
  • You're not ready to build something people follow

We're a perfect fit if:

  • You believe attention is earned before it's amplified
  • You want a community, not just a campaign
  • You trust research, creative, then ads, in that order
  • You're ready to invest in an audience that compounds
  • You want die-hard fans, not rented eyeballs
