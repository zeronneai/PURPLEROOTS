Frequently Asked Questions

What is Purple Roots Agency?

Purple Roots Agency is a cinema-grade video production and creative marketing agency based in El Paso, Texas. Led by founder and creative director Nadim Athie, the agency helps businesses turn attention into sales through short-form video, commercials, brand strategy, influencer marketing, and AI-augmented services.

Where is Purple Roots Agency located?

Purple Roots Agency is located at WestStar Tower, 601 N Mesa St, Suite 1440, El Paso, TX 79901. It serves clients in El Paso, Las Cruces, Juarez, and across the United States.

What services does Purple Roots Agency offer?

Video production (brand films, commercials, short-form content for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube), social media management, brand identity and rebrands, influencer marketing, web design and development, email and SMS marketing, paid media amplification, and AI-augmented marketing services.

What makes Purple Roots Agency different from other El Paso video production companies?

Purple Roots combines cinema-grade production quality with a comedy-driven short-form formula and full-funnel marketing strategy. Its stated method is "attention first": research the market, earn attention with creative content, build community, then amplify proven content with paid ads. The agency reports its content formula has generated 700M+ organic views across client work.
