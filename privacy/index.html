Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Purple Roots Agency ("Purple Roots", "we", "us") collects through purpleroots.agency and our forms, how we use it, and the choices you have. Purple Roots Agency is located at WestStar Tower, 601 N Mesa St, Suite 1440, El Paso, TX 79901.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us when you request an audit or contact us: your name, business name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, social media account, and anything you write in the message.
  • Your text message consent, if you choose to give it: whether you checked the SMS box, the exact wording you agreed to, the date and time, and the page where you agreed.
  • Basic usage data: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits in aggregate. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
  • Security checks: our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam bots. Cloudflare may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details for that purpose only.

How we use your information

  • To prepare and send the audit you requested and to answer your inquiry.
  • To contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp about your request.
  • To send you text messages about your request, only if you checked the SMS consent box. See our SMS Terms.
  • To keep our forms and website secure and free of spam.

Text messaging and your mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with the service providers that help us send messages, and only to deliver them.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Google (email and spreadsheets), Cloudflare (spam protection), Vercel (website hosting) and our text messaging provider, and only so they can perform those services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you and to work with you, and to keep records of your messaging consent as required by law. You can ask us to delete it at any time, except where we must keep it to comply with the law.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing p.rootsagency@gmail.com or calling +1 (915) 234-3655.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
